Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

OneRoute helps a group of drivers stay together. This page says, in plain words, what it collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password (stored only as a salted hash). A profile photo is optional. If you choose Google sign-in, Google tells us your name and verified email address (never your Google password).
  • During a trip: your live location and speed, your name and photo, and any messages, voice clips, stops or SOS alerts you send.
  • Consent record: the date you agreed to the Terms.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not collect contacts or device or advertising IDs.

Who can see it

Everyone in your trip sees your name, photo, location and messages. Anyone with the trip’s read-only live link can see names, photos and locations too, so share that link only with people you trust.

Other services

The map code comes from our own server. To work, the app sends limited data to OpenStreetMap services (map tiles, place search), the OSRM routing service (start and destination points) the Overpass API (the area searched for nearby places, and the area around an SOS to find nearby help) and Open-Meteo (the coordinates of your stops and destination, for weather). If you switch on satellite view, map images load from Esri. If you choose “Continue with Google”, the sign-in button is loaded from Google, which then sees your visit. Those services also see your IP address. Each has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

  • Account details and photo: until you delete your account.
  • Trip data (locations, messages, voice clips): held in server memory only, never saved to disk. It is erased when a trip has had nobody online for 6 hours, or when the server restarts.
  • Saved sign-ins: up to a year, renewed each time you use the app, or 1 day if you did not tick “Keep me signed in”.

Your choices

You can stop sharing at any time with “Stop sharing & leave”, change or remove your photo, refuse location permission in your browser, or delete your account from the start screen. To ask for a copy, a correction or erasure of your data, or to make a complaint, email akshayshankar017@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days. Where the law gives you privacy rights (for example, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), they apply.

Security and age

Passwords are hashed and access is limited, but no online service is perfectly secure. OneRoute is for people aged 18 or over.
