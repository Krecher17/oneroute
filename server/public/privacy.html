Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Information we handle

OneRoute may process account details such as name and email address, password hashes, profile photos, trip codes, route and destination information, live GPS coordinates, convoy status, messages or voice clips, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service. Only collect fields required for the features a person chooses to use.

2. Location and convoy sharing

Location is sensitive. When a person joins an active trip and grants device permission, their location and trip status may be shared with other members of that convoy. A separate read-only live link may let anyone who possesses the link view trip activity. Share it only with trusted people. Provide a clear way to stop sharing, leave a trip, revoke live links, and delete trip data according to the production system's capabilities.

3. Why information is used

  • Create and secure accounts and keep users signed in when they choose that option.
  • Provide routing, live convoy tracking, missed-turn alerts, navigation, SOS, chat and voice features.
  • Prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and protect service reliability.
  • Meet legal obligations where applicable.

Do not use precise location or account data for advertising or unrelated profiling unless separately disclosed and lawfully enabled.

4. Providers and international transfers

OneRoute currently uses or may use hosting and routing providers, MapLibre assets from a CDN, OpenStreetMap map tiles, and OpenStreetMap's Overpass API for nearby-place searches. These providers receive request data as described in their own policies. Confirm the actual providers, hosting region, and terms before launch; do not imply that all processing stays on your server.

5. Storage and security

Passwords should be stored only as strong salted hashes. Session tokens, account files, uploaded photos, logs and backups must be protected from public access. Use HTTPS, access controls, upload validation, rate limits and appropriate retention limits. No online service can promise perfect security.

6. Retention and deletion

Keep account, location, trip, message, voice and log data only as long as needed for the stated purpose, security, or legal requirements. Publish actual retention periods and provide a process to request access, correction or deletion. Leaving a trip should stop future sharing; it may not automatically erase past records unless implemented to do so.

7. Children

OneRoute is not intended for children who cannot lawfully consent to this service. If the service is made available to minors, implement the age-related safeguards and consent required by applicable law.

8. Your choices and rights

Users can manage device location permissions, choose whether to remain signed in, remove a profile photo if supported, stop sharing and contact us about their data. Applicable privacy rights depend on the user's location and the law.
