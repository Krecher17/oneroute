Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

OneRoute sets no cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for the items below, only to make the app work or to remember what you chose.

What is stored

  • oneroute-token keeps you signed in. It lasts up to a year (renewed each time you use the app), or until you close the tab if you did not tick “Keep me signed in”.
  • oneroute-alerts and oneroute-navvoice remember your sound and spoken-direction settings.
  • oneroute-loc-ok remembers that you read the location-sharing notice.

Consent

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking items, so there is no cookie banner. These items are needed for the service you asked for. If we ever add anything optional, we will ask first.

If you use the “Continue with Google” button, Google’s own script and cookies on google.com apply to that button.

Clearing it

Use “Sign out” in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings.

Third parties

Map tiles and place searches come from OpenStreetMap services, which can see your IP address. See the Privacy Policy.
